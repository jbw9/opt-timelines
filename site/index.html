Decode your own silent update

A silent update is when the case data behind your myUSCIS account changes but the status message doesn't. It often comes a few days before an approval, but it isn't one.

  1. Sign in at my.uscis.gov.
  2. In the same browser, open https://my.uscis.gov/account/case-service/api/cases/ followed by your receipt number, for example …/cases/IOE0912345678.
  3. You'll see a page of text (JSON). Copy all of it and paste it below. It stays in this page and is never sent anywhere.

Check the updatedAt time: if it changed but your status message didn't, that's a silent update.

Hover over any mark for dates, and click it to open the Reddit comment it came from.
